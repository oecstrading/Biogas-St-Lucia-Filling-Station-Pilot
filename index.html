ℹ️ Executive Summary: This pilot integrates a 6 m³ modular digester with a new filling station in the Gros Islet/Rodney Bay corridor. It leverages the Deglos Sanitary Landfill catchment — which receives 5,500 tonnes/month with 50% organic content — and the dense hotel belt (12+ hotels). It produces 4.5 m³/day of biogas, compresses to 200 bar into 30 CNG-rated cylinders for 20 households and 1 commercial kitchen (hotel/resort). At commercial scale (500 cylinders), payback is 1.3 years with 44% IRR.

🚨 FATAL DESIGN CONSTRAINT: Standard LPG cylinders (20 lb & 100 lb) are pressure-rated to 17–25 bar working pressure. Compressed biogas requires 200–250 bar to achieve practical energy density. Standard LPG cylinders CANNOT be used for compressed biogas.

✅ Feedstock Security: The Deglos Landfill receives 5,500 tonnes of waste per month, of which approximately 50% is organic material (food waste 69%, yard waste 24%, agricultural residue 7%)[reference:16]. Waste audits of eight participating hotels showed 56.7% of hotel solid waste is organic, with food scraps comprising 79.3% of that organic matter[reference:17]. This provides a secure, year-round feedstock supply for the pilot.
